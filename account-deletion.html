Delete your FitPie account

FitPie (developer: FitPie)

In the app

  1. Open FitPie and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately (Apple sign-in users confirm with Apple once more).

Without the app

Email xsdc1456@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking to delete your account. We verify the request and delete the account within 10 days.

What is deleted

  • Deleted immediately and permanently: account (email, name), workout, food and body records, profile, settings, templates, custom foods and exercises.
  • Kept up to 3 years, then deleted automatically: a one-way hash of your sign-in identity and your free-trial start date, used only to prevent repeating the free week.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
